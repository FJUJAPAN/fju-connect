+20 points per activity

+50 points

Important rules

📍 In-person check-in

Check-in is available only near your registered church: within a 100-meter radius, with accurate GPS location. One check-in per day is allowed at each church.

✅ Activities require approval

Church cleaning, evangelism, and registering a new or returning person must be submitted as requests. Points are added only after coordinator approval.

🗓️ Follow-up period for an evangelized person

The first approved registration starts a one-month follow-up period for that person. Points are counted in the regular monthly ranking.

🏆 Weekly and monthly rankings

The weekly ranking runs from Monday through Sunday. The monthly ranking tracks points earned during the month. Check your national and local polo rankings.

🤝 Participate responsibly

Points recognize real participation. Do not check in when you are away from the church or request points for activities you did not complete. Irregular records may be rejected.
